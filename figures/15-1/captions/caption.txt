Figure 15-1: Racist, sexist, and homophobic opinions, US, 1987-2012. Values are transcribed from the public Pew Research Center 2012 Values Survey topline tables for the matching question responses (Q41e, Q41j, and Q40k), not from the Pinker chart. The extended panel contains no post-2012 line because a comparable continuation was not identified. Visual inspection suggests that the source chart's top light line follows the school-board response while its black line follows the women response; this apparent label/trajectory inversion is retained as an unresolved source-figure anomaly.
