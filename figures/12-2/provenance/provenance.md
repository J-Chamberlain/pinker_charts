# Provenance

## Book source line

United States: FBI Uniform Crime Reports and Federal Bureau of Investigation 2016. England including Wales: Office for National Statistics 2017. World, 2000: Krug et al. 2002. World, 2003-2011: UN Economic and Social Council 2014, fig. 1; percentages converted using a 2012 rate of 6.2 from UNODC 2014.

## Downloads

- BJS/FBI table: https://bjs.ojp.gov/content/pub/pdf/htius.pdf
- OWID/UNODC rates: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/homicide-rate-unodc.csv

## Transformations

The BJS PDF table is text-extracted programmatically into yearly US rates for 1967-2005. OWID/UNODC rates are used for the US overlap, England, and World through 2015, then as a dashed successor through 2023. No plotted values were digitized.
