Figure 5-3: Maternal mortality, 1751-2013. Reconstructed from preserved Our World in Data dataset 522, which combines Gapminder (2010) historical values with World Bank (2015) estimates without adjustment. Source ratios per 100,000 live births are divided by 1,000 to reproduce the book's percent axis. The comparison's left panel is an evidence-based facsimile because the located Supplemental Graphics host blocked direct PDF retrieval; it is not an original page crop. The dashed 2014-2015 segment is a same-source continuation. No current OWID values are appended because the successor uses revised UN/WHO sources and processing.
