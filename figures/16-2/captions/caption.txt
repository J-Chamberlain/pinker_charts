Figure16-2. Basic education,1820-2010. Archived OWID data recover nine original regional/World series, cross-checked against OECD2014 Table5.3. World1820 is a model-based backcast; remaining observations are decadal estimates, not annual surveys. Lines connect available records; Eastern Europe ends1990. Original XLS2000/2010 row labels are reversed; the printed publication and archived CSV agree on the chronology used here. Small vertical differences from the book remain; no values were shifted to fit pixels. Historical schooling coverage is not proof of literacy, learning, or primary completion. The extended comparison has no observed extension: today's OWID successor uses different regions and source methods, reports conflicting age metadata, and includes projections from2015 onward. Those projections are retained only in the diagnostic, not presented as observed progress.
