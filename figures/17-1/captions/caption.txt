Work hours for full-time production workers, 1870-2000. Reconstructed from Huberman-Minns Table 1 via retained OWID data; Western Europe uses the unweighted Old World aggregate. The source-backed lines appear roughly one hour above the book, an unresolved assembly/plotting difference; no offset was fitted. The year-2000 values are equal means of male and female estimates. No comparable extension is plotted: current same-variable data stop in 2000, and all-worker successors change the population. The extension review panel repeats the book-period reconstruction explicitly.
