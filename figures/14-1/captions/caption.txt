Figure 14-1: Democracy versus autocracy, 1800-2015. Partial reconstruction using 216 numerical World averages from the cited HumanProgress dataset 2560 (July 2016 update), based on Polity IV 2015. Broad trajectory and 2008 arrow match; mid-century deviations remain. The provider's generated-value flags and changing country coverage are preserved, not treated as independently observed global scores. Despite the book's wording, this is an average, not a sum or population-weighted mean. Separate successor panel: Polity IV/5 2018 polity2 averages, dotted overlapping history and dashed 2016-2018; different coverage and missing-value handling prevent a seamless join.
