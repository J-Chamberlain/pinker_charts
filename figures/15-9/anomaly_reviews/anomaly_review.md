# Figure 15-9 anomaly review

## Major

- Definitions and age bands differ across the five source series. The source note and
  caption preserve this limitation instead of combining them into a falsely harmonized
  measure.
- OWID metadata attributes the US historical values to Long (1958), while Pinker's
  source note cites Whaples (2005). The historical values match the public OWID table,
  but the exact source-version handoff is not byte-verified.

## Minor

- Line colors, label positions, and typography are approximations of the reference.
- The extended output has no dashed continuation because no comparable post-2012 source
  was accepted; this is stated on the plot and in the caption.

## Decision

Status: `verified_reproduction` for the source-backed 1850-2012 book-period series,
with extension unavailable and source-version caveat documented.
