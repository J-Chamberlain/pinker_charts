Figure 16-1. Literacy, 1475-2010. The actual OWID CSV archived March 25, 2016 supplies all eight original series. Lines connect sparse observations, not annual measurements. The World dip and plateau are preserved from that dataset, not evidence of independently verified historical reversals. Later OWID versions revise these values substantially. US 1870-1979 values agree with NCES's basic literacy measure for age 14+, not functional literacy. Early European estimates and later adult surveys have different definitions and coverage. The extended comparison retains the frozen book panel and adds a separate revised OWID/UNESCO UIS panel: dotted overlap through 2010 and dashed later observations. It is not a seamless continuation. Chile ends in 2017, Italy in 2019, Mexico and World in 2024; the other four series have no comparable extension here. Typography differs slightly from the book; no values were digitized or adjusted to improve visual fit.
