Figure 16-5. IQ gains, 1909-2013. Six regional/World trajectories use OWID's July 2015 numerical extraction of Pietschnig & Voracek (2015). These are cumulative test-score changes from different starting dates, not comparable regional IQ levels or evidence about inherited group differences. Lines join source knots; no chart values were digitized. Europe's initial zero anchor remains in the data but is omitted from display as in the original. The original supplement's weighting and regional aggregation are not independently verified, so the figure remains partial despite close visual agreement. No comparable extension has been recovered; the extended comparison explicitly repeats history. Later studies with different tests or samples are not spliced onto the curves.
