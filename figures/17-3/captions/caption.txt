Eight utility/appliance series use Greenwood et al.'s original workbook, with the book's 2005/2011 Census updates. The housework curve is incomplete: only the published 1900/1975 anchors are shown, without an invented connecting trajectory or 2015 endpoint. The early stove series measures electric ranges; later values include gas stoves, so they are not joined. The original housework estimates have been challenged in later research. No comparable extension is established; the extended review repeats this partial reconstruction. Modern BLS candidates are preserved separately, not passed off as the book series.
