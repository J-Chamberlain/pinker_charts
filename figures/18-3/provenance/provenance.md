The series has not been extended because the data are not commensurable with current records. Switzerland, 1880-1959: Ajdacic-Gross et al. 2006, fig. 1. The plotted unit is suicides per 100,000 people. The chart combines three country series with different source vintages and explicitly declines to extend the England/Wales series because current records are not commensurable.
