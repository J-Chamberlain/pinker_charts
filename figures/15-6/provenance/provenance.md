# Figure 15-6 provenance

## Original source note

Welzel 2013, fig. 4.1. World Values Survey data are from Australia, Canada, France, West Germany, Italy, Japan, the Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, the United Kingdom, and the United States, with each country weighted equally.

## Source chain

The cited figure is a secondary rendering of the emancipative-values index described in Christian Welzel, *Freedom Rising* (2013), figure 4.1. The World Values Survey publishes Welzel's index construction and identifies the underlying items as autonomy, equality, choice, and voice. The WVS archive also publishes integrated WVS/EVS time-series data documentation. Leuphana's publication record points to the Welzel replication archive at GESIS, ZA7470, DOI 10.4232/1.12407.

## Current recovery state

The original figure image is stored at `references/figures/figure_15_6.png`. No plotted values were digitized. The respondent-level integrated file or Welzel's derived cohort data were not downloaded because the available WVS data route requires registration and non-redistribution terms, while the GESIS record was not accessible as a direct machine download in this run. The figure remains blocked at source recovery.
