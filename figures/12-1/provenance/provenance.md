# Provenance

## Book source line

England, Netherlands & Belgium, Italy, 1300-1994: Eisner 2003, plotted in fig. 3-3 of Pinker 2011. England, 2000-2014: UK Office for National Statistics. Italy and Netherlands, 2010-2012: United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime 2014. New England and Southwest US: Roth 2009, plotted in figs. 3-13 and 3-16 of Pinker 2011. Mexico: Carlos Vilalta, personal communication, originally from INEGI 2016 and Botello 2016.

## Downloaded public data

- OWID historical Europe import: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/homicide-rates-across-western-europe.csv
- OWID/UNODC current rates: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/homicide-rate-unodc.csv

## Transformation

The OWID Europe table is filtered to England and Wales, Italy, Netherlands, and Belgium. Netherlands and Belgium are averaged by year as a transparent approximation to the original combined line. Mexico is retained from the UNODC series from 1990 onward. Modern post-2015 data are separate dashed successor series. No values were digitized from Pinker's plot.
