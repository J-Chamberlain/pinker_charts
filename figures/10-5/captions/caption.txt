Figure 10-5: Oil spills, 1970-2016. This is a partial reconstruction. The
black line plots verified OWID/ITOPF tanker oil-spill counts of at least 7
metric tons for 1970-2016. The book's gray oil-shipped-by-sea line is not
plotted because the annual 1970-2016 Roser 2016r/ITOPF/UNCTADStat source was
not recovered. A current UNCTADStat US.SeaborneTrade bulk file was recovered
and archived in this repository, but it is labeled "From 2000 to 2024" and its
World cargo 11+12 values differ from UNCTAD Review of Maritime Transport 2020
selected-year tanker-trade values by about 31-48 percent on overlap, so it is
not used as a comparable substitute. The extended artifact carries forward only
the same OWID/ITOPF spill-count series through 2025; no oil-shipping successor
line is plotted.
