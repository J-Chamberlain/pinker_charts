Figure 10-5: Oil spills, 1970-2016. The black line shows annual tanker oil spills of at least 7 tonnes from ITOPF/OWID. The gray oil-shipped-by-sea line is digitized from an archived 2016 ITOPF chart image that cites UNCTADStat for total crude oil, petroleum product, and gas loaded; it is not recovered from the original annual UNCTADStat table. No post-2016 extension is plotted because a same-method annual successor series has not been recovered.
