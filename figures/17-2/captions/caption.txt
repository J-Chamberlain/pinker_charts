Figure 17-2. Retirement, US, 1880-2010. Men age 65+ participating in the labor force, percent. Historical values: Costa (1998), Table 2A.1, gainful employment; 2000: Short (2002), 17.5%; 2010: original BLS annual Table 3, 22.1%. The historical definition differs from CPS survey-week participation. The extension uses BLS annual averages for 2011-2024 (May 2025 release), dashed, with a matching 2010 anchor. The overall shape closely matches the book, but small level/position differences and the author's exact assembly remain unresolved. Source numbers were not shifted to fit the reference. Nonparticipation is not synonymous with retirement; this is not an estimate of retirement income or adequacy.
