Figure 18-4: Happiness and excitement, US, 1972-2016. The reconstruction uses NORC's public cumulative General Social Survey release, calculating the percentage of valid responses coded “very happy” (HAPPY = 1) and “life is exciting” (LIFE = 1). Solid lines show the book period; dashed lines show the comparable continuation available in the current cumulative release. The current release is an institutional successor, not the exact 2015/2016 extract cited in the book, and its LIFE question is observed only in selected survey years. Percentages exclude nonresponses.
