Figure 18-4. Happiness and excitement, US. Solid lines show unweighted valid-response percentages from NORC GSS release 3a for 1972-2016, a close candidate but not a proven copy of the original Data Explorer export. Lines join observed years; no missing-year values are invented. Original report tables and alternative weights are retained as diagnostics. Dashed lines show a separately WTSSPS-weighted successor through 2024, with a 2016 overlap. The 2018-2021 gap marks the survey-mode break; the later decline is not a proven homogeneous continuation.
