## Acceptance and rejection

The current NORC release was accepted for an updated-equivalent reconstruction because it is the same institution and survey family and contains the required variables. It was not accepted as the original historical dataset because the 2015 report percentages do not exactly equal the current-release percentages and the cited 2016 project extract was not recovered.
