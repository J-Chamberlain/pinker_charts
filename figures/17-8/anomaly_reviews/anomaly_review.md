Reviewer: Codex direct self-review, 2026-09-09. Not an external independent audit.
Original full-resolution PDF crop, both reconstruction PNGs, both side-by-side

## Visible differences and iteration

The original and reconstruction share the 0.5-1.3 billion scale, 1995-2015 date
range and the same prominent bends: the 2000-2001 plateau, 2003 dip, 2004 rise
and 2009 dip. Plot areas are close in size and neither reference is a placeholder.
The final segment is dotted and the endpoint hollow, unlike the original solid
line. This is intentional disclosure of a different source vintage, not a
styling oversight. The typography is a substitute, not a pixel-identical font.

The extended chart changes its y minimum to 0.3 billion to include the pandemic
collapse. Its historical segment is horizontally compressed by the longer
1995-2025 time range. Both are necessary, captioned changes. Gray and teal dashed
segments identify different UN Tourism vintages, with no fabricated bridge
between 2018 and 2019. The 2015 successor overlap is retained separately rather
than normalized to the historical endpoint. The pandemic annotation was moved
left after visual inspection to avoid the descending line; diagnostic year ticks
were changed from fractional years to calendar years.

## Severity / editorial gate

- Critical: none in this review package. Real reference, reconstruction, data,
  scripts, units and captions are present. Exact vintage is not falsely claimed.
- Major, explained: missing original-vintage 2015 endpoint. Located 2016 and
  January 2017 files end in 2014; May/October 2017 revise the history. Substitution
  is drawn explicitly and prevents verified status.
- Major, explained: successor datasets are not identical World Bank aggregates.
  Separate dashed segments and source-version columns prevent a silent splice.
- Minor: substitute fonts; unavoidable smaller type for the longer extended
  caption. All remain readable. Annotation overlap and diagnostic ticks corrected.

The package is suitable for transparent research review as **partial_match**,
not a claim of exact reconstruction. Publication remains incomplete pending the
historical endpoint resolution and project-level reference-image rights review.
No unresolved Critical issue and no unexplained Major issue remain on the pages.

## Reviewer challenge

1. **Pinker:** Why is the final point hollow? The full book-era input is not
   proven; the 2017 endpoint is disclosed. Action: targeted original-download
   recovery, not visual fitting. Documented/new research task.
2. **Data journalist:** Are these people or trips, and why the pandemic gap?
   Arrivals are trips; repeat travelers count repeatedly. The 2020 collapse is
   directly in the UN source table. Definitions and provisional 2025 explained.
3. **Peer reviewer:** Can the same total be obtained across source releases?
   No blanket comparability claim. Retained WDI/UN versions, row selection,
   region completeness checks and a rejected-source diagnostic make this auditable.
4. **Skeptical reader:** Why not simply use the current API? It changes historical
   World totals dramatically. That failed approach is retained as evidence and
   excluded from chart input, not quietly scaled down. Resolved for this package.
