# Provenance: Figure 10-2

Supplemental Graphics PDF page 13 -> official XKCD 1007 page/image -> Google Ngram API for `sustainable` in American English -> `scripts/reconstruct_10_2.py` -> book-period and extended comparison plots.

The book-period reconstruction uses the Google Ngram American English 2009/v1 corpus through 2008 plus a log-linear extrapolation to the XKCD horizon. The extended reconstruction overlays current Ngram successor observations through 2019 and a separate successor fit.
