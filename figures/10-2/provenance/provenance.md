The observed book-period dots use the Google Ngram American English 2009/v1 corpus through 2008. The solid projection and future open-circle markers use a single least-squares log-linear visual calibration to the XKCD future labels, so the 2036 marker is no longer independently hard-coded. A separate Ngram-data-only fit is retained as a dotted diagnostic line and in the candidate comparison table. The extended reconstruction overlays current Ngram successor observations through 2019 and a separate successor fit.
