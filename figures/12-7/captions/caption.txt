Figure 12-7: Occupational accident deaths, US, 1913-2015. Recreated from published BLS, National Safety Council, CDC/NIOSH, OSHA, NCHS, and CFOI observations named in the book's source note. The 2006-2015 rates are BLS FTE rates multiplied by .95 as described by Pinker. The dashed 2016 point is a successor observation. Because source definitions and denominators change, this is a partial reconstruction, not a homogeneous verified series.
