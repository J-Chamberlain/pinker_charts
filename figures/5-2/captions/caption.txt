Figure 5-2: Child mortality, 1751–2013. Documented partial match, not a verified reconstruction. The book cites Our World in Data, Roser 2016a, based on UN Child Mortality Estimates and the Human Mortality Database. An immutable OWID copy of the contemporaneous CME Info (2016) input was recovered, but it begins in 1970 and the complete UN/HMD chart assembly was not. The plotted values are therefore the current OWID successor in percent units, clipped to the five lines and source starts visible in the book. No post-2013 extension is plotted because the current Gapminder/UN-IGME source chain is not demonstrably comparable to the cited 2016 UN/HMD assembly.
